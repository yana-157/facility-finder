Data & methods

Recommended ranking

TravelTime estimates typical weekday-morning drives of up to three hours. Recommended adds penalties for service match and evidence confidence to the driving minutes. If routing is unavailable, the search labels and uses straight-line distance.

Projected load

Load balancing is off by default. Turn it on to compare nearby branches with similar evidence for the same service. The calculation gives equal weight to extra travel and workload pressure, using monthly caseload and modeled throughput. It can change which branch ranks first.

Published snapshot

Service evidence and reported or estimated bed counts come from the dated facility release. Call the branch to confirm current availability before traveling.
